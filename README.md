## A Software Dev Demo's His Project to His Dad

The screen glows blue in the kitchen light,  
He pulls up GitHub, eager, bright.  
“Check it out, Dad — it’s live, it’s real!  
Three hundred stars, a Solar feel.”

Dad squints at commits, at CI green,  
At Dockerfiles he's never seen.  
“You type all that? For what — a blog?”  
“No, no, it’s infra, like a cog!”

He clicks the URL, clouds align,  
A modal pops — “Hey, that looks fine.”  
“It’s React,” the dev says, hope aflame.  
“Your uncle Jim still uses Frame.”

He demos the cache, the edge deploy,  
Dad nods along with cautious joy.  
“So people pay?” “Well, not just yet...”  
“But seed is raised! Don’t fret, don’t fret.”

Dad pours more coffee, slow and hot,  
Proud, though he understands it not.  
“My boy built this?” He views the rows  
Of neon code. “That’s something, Joe.”

“It’s Joel,” he sighs, but smiles wide —  
No linter catching father pride.
